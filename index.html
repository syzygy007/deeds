    Not For Followers.

    We said it. Then we built a follow button. Both are true.

    Chase the count and it goes hollow. Chase the deed and the count takes care of itself.

    GOOD COMPANIES.

    Companies whose people turn good intentions into measurable action.
    Counting the good...
    Recognition is earned annually.
    Did this organization cause a meaningful amount of good to happen?

    That is the whole standard. Companies do not tell DEEDS. how much good they do. DEEDS. counts it: one qualifying deed by one verified person is one deed. No essays, no donation totals, no volunteer hours, no weighting.

    Every company gets an annual goal sized to its people, and recognition is earned again every year.

    You cannot buy your way onto the list. You can only do your way onto it.
