Guidelines.

What belongs here, and what does not.

Effective August 24, 2026

Why this page exists.

These Community Guidelines describe what is and is not permitted on DEEDS.

They supplement our Terms of Use and apply to all accounts, posts, deeds, comments, messages, publications, photographs, videos, profiles, and other activity on DEEDS. If these Guidelines conflict with our Terms of Use, the Terms of Use control.

DEEDS has one rule underneath all the others. Leave the place better than you found it.

The rules below exist to protect the people using DEEDS, the integrity of what is recorded here, and the purpose of the platform.

What belongs here.

A deed you actually did. Small is fine. Small is most of it.

You may share the work, the reason, the person who taught you, something you learned, something you made, something you gave, or something you are reading.

You may encourage someone else, pass a deed forward, share an idea, publish thoughtful work, or invite someone to participate.

Whatever you post should be genuine and consistent with the purpose of DEEDS.

Identity and privacy.

DEEDS allows people to participate without exposing their identity. Respect that.

  • Do not reveal, attempt to discover, or help others discover the identity of a member who has chosen to remain anonymous.
  • Do not post another person's name, photograph, address, telephone number, email address, precise location, employer, social media account, financial information, identification documents, or other private or identifying information without appropriate authorization.
  • Do not use clues, photographs, contextual details, external links, or other information to circumvent another member's choice to remain anonymous.
  • Do not encourage others to identify, locate, investigate, contact, or expose another member.

Photographs and recordings of other people.

Think before posting another person. A photograph of someone you helped can turn your good deed into their unwanted exposure.

Do not post photographs, video, audio, or other recordings of another person unless you have the rights and permissions necessary to do so. When appropriate, obtain permission before posting.

If you cannot obtain permission, photograph the meal, the letter, the supplies, the work, the location, the empty chair, or something else that tells the story without identifying the person.

By posting content to DEEDS, you represent that you have all rights, licenses, permissions, and consents reasonably necessary to post that content.

Money and solicitation.

DEEDS is not a fundraising or payment platform.

  • Do not use DEEDS to solicit money, charitable contributions, investments, loans, tips, gifts, goods, services, or other things of value.
  • Do not post payment links, crowdfunding campaigns, commercial solicitations, referral schemes, pyramid schemes, or requests for direct financial assistance.
  • Do not disguise advertising, solicitation, or commercial promotion as a deed.

Harm, threats, and harassment.

  • Do not threaten, harass, stalk, intimidate, exploit, bully, or target another person.
  • Do not encourage violence or publish credible threats of violence.
  • Do not publish private information for the purpose of exposing, embarrassing, intimidating, or harming another person.
  • Do not encourage suicide, self harm, eating disorders, or conduct that creates a material risk of physical harm.
  • Do not use DEEDS to coordinate, facilitate, encourage, or promote unlawful conduct.

Sexual and explicit content.

Pornographic and sexually explicit content is not permitted. Nonconsensual intimate imagery is prohibited.

This includes authentic photographs or videos as well as altered, synthetic, manipulated, or AI generated depictions of an identifiable person.

Graphic violence, gore, and content primarily intended to shock, disturb, or traumatize are also prohibited.

Nonconsensual intimate imagery removal requests.

If an intimate photograph, video, or other covered visual depiction of you has been posted on DEEDS without your consent, you or a person authorized to act on your behalf may request its removal.

Send the request to:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature, or the signature of the person authorized to act on your behalf.
  2. Information reasonably sufficient for us to identify and locate the image or video, such as the URL, post, account, screenshot, or other identifying information.
  3. A brief statement that you have a good faith belief that the identified intimate depiction was published without your consent, together with any relevant information that may assist us in evaluating the request.
  4. Information reasonably sufficient for us to contact you or your authorized representative.
takeitdown@deeds.fun

Your written request should include:

Upon receiving a valid removal request covered by applicable law, DEEDS will remove the reported material as soon as reasonably possible and within the time required by law. Where required, we will also make reasonable efforts to identify and remove known identical copies. You do not need to have a DEEDS account to submit a request. Submitting a knowingly false or fraudulent request is prohibited.

Minors.

Any sexual exploitation or abuse of minors is strictly prohibited.

This includes child sexual abuse material, grooming, sexual solicitation, sexual exploitation, sexualized depictions of minors, and attempts to obtain or distribute such material.

Content or conduct involving apparent child sexual exploitation may be removed immediately, accounts may be suspended or terminated without warning, and information may be preserved or reported to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children, law enforcement, or other appropriate authorities when required or permitted by law.

DEEDS may take additional measures when necessary to protect minors.

Hate and discriminatory abuse.

Do not attack, threaten, degrade, dehumanize, or advocate violence against people based on protected characteristics.

Do not use DEEDS to promote hatred, exclusion, or violence directed at a protected group.

Discussion of difficult subjects is permitted. Targeted abuse is not.

Spam, advertising, and manipulation.

Do not spam.

  • Do not mass post substantially identical content, operate coordinated inauthentic accounts, send unsolicited bulk messages, or use automation in a way that disrupts the platform.
  • Do not buy, sell, trade, manufacture, or artificially inflate deeds, reactions, engagement, account activity, or other platform metrics.
  • Do not use misleading techniques intended primarily to generate attention or engagement.
  • Do not advertise or promote unrelated commercial products or services unless DEEDS expressly permits that activity.

Authenticity.

Do not log deeds you did not do.

Do not fabricate events, falsify evidence, manipulate records, impersonate another person or organization, or knowingly create a materially false impression about something that occurred.

Do not manufacture deeds solely to manipulate rankings, recognition, challenges, company statistics, or other measurements on DEEDS.

The usefulness of the platform depends on the integrity of what people record. A count nobody can trust is worth nothing.

Impersonation and deceptive behavior.

Do not impersonate another person, company, nonprofit organization, government entity, or other organization in a misleading manner.

Do not create accounts intended to deceive people about who is responsible for them.

Parody, commentary, or other lawful expression must not be presented in a manner reasonably likely to mislead people about the source of the account or content.

Intellectual property.

Post only content you created or have the right to use.

Do not post material that infringes or misappropriates another person's copyright, trademark, publicity rights, or other intellectual property rights.

Copyright owners and their authorized representatives may submit copyright notices to:

copyright@deeds.fun

Additional requirements and procedures are described in our Copyright Policy.

Platform integrity and security.

Do not interfere with the operation or security of DEEDS.

  • Do not attempt to gain unauthorized access to accounts, systems, data, or nonpublic areas of the service.
  • Do not introduce malware, malicious code, automated attacks, scraping systems prohibited by our Terms, or tools intended to circumvent security or access controls.
  • Do not misuse reporting, blocking, moderation, authentication, or other platform systems.

Reporting.

If you believe content or conduct violates these Guidelines, use the report function available on DEEDS.

For safety related reports, you may also contact:

safety@deeds.fun

Reports may be evaluated through automated systems, human review, or both.

Do not knowingly submit false, misleading, fraudulent, repetitive, or abusive reports. Submitting a report does not guarantee that content will be removed or that an account will be restricted.

Blocking.

Where available, you may block another member using the controls provided by DEEDS.

Blocking is a personal account control and does not necessarily constitute a determination that the blocked member violated these Guidelines.

Enforcement.

Unilateral Good LLC reserves the right to investigate suspected violations and take action when we believe content, conduct, accounts, or activity violate these Guidelines, our Terms of Use, other applicable policies, applicable law, or present a risk to DEEDS, its members, or others.

Depending on the circumstances, we may:

  • remove content;
  • restrict distribution or visibility;
  • disable features;
  • issue warnings;
  • require additional verification;
  • limit account functionality;
  • temporarily suspend an account;
  • permanently terminate an account;
  • prevent the creation of additional accounts;
  • preserve information;
  • cooperate with lawful governmental or legal requests; or
  • take other action we consider reasonably appropriate.

We may take action with or without advance notice where appropriate.

Enforcement decisions may consider the nature and severity of the conduct, context, account history, risk of harm, attempts to evade enforcement, legal requirements, and other relevant circumstances.

DEEDS is not required to monitor every account, communication, or piece of content. The fact that content remains available does not mean that DEEDS has reviewed, approved, endorsed, or determined that content complies with these Guidelines.

Failure to enforce a provision in one circumstance does not waive our right to enforce it in another.

Appeals.

If DEEDS provides an opportunity to appeal an enforcement decision, you may submit an appeal through the process provided with that decision or contact:

appeals@deeds.fun

We may request additional information reasonably necessary to evaluate an appeal. Submitting an appeal does not guarantee reinstatement of content, features, or an account.

One last thing.

There are no followers worth chasing here and no metric worth gaming.

If you find yourself performing rather than doing, close the app and go do something small for someone.

Not for recognition. Not for followers. For the next deed.
