How moderation works.

Most content publishes immediately. We screen automatically as it goes up, and severe material is stopped before it appears. Everything else is reviewed after the fact, and anything that breaks the rules is taken down.

Repeated violations accumulate and can lead to a temporary suspension or a permanent removal. The full range of what we may do is set out under Enforcement in the Community Guidelines.

If we remove something of yours and you believe we were wrong, write to appeals@deeds.fun and a person will look at it again.

Counts, ranks, and organizations.

DEEDS keeps counts. Deeds logged, redeeds caused, network reach, organization totals. These are records of participation, not certified facts. We do not independently verify that a logged deed actually happened.

Organizations may register and be verified. Verification confirms that the organization is real and that the person who registered it is entitled to. It is not an endorsement, and it is not a statement about anything the organization does elsewhere.

Attempting to manipulate counts, invites, rankings, or campaign totals is grounds for removal of the account and the organization behind it.
